# Disclaimer

Cord is a protocol for verifying the **integrity** of AI-generated outputs.

It ensures that data has not been modified after generation by using deterministic hashing and verifiable evidence structures.

---

## No Guarantee of Correctness

Cord does **not** guarantee that:

- AI outputs are accurate
- AI outputs are correct or complete
- AI outputs comply with any policy, regulation, or agreement
- a specific model actually produced the output (without additional authentication mechanisms)

Cord only verifies that the provided data is internally consistent and has not been altered.
