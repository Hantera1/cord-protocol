# Cord Trade — Arabic Market Launch Brief (Working Draft)

## Positioning
**Cord Trade — Evidence and licensing infrastructure for commercial agreements.**

Cord Trade helps businesses create, manage and verify evidence connected to trade agreements, counterparties, assets, payment references and delivery events. It does not replace legal counsel, a bank, an exchange, a government registry or a licensed electronic trust-service provider.

## Initial UAE offer
Start with a controlled, non-exclusive pilot for one UAE-based reseller or implementation partner. The partner receives a territory-limited reseller license, approved Arabic/English materials, onboarding and a sales reporting obligation. No sublicensing or exclusivity by default.

## Target customer profiles
- luxury watch and jewellery dealers;
- precious-metal and bullion traders;
- auction houses and professional resellers;
- import/export and wholesale businesses;
- B2B platforms and document/workflow integrators.

## Arabic product package
- Arabic right-to-left product interface and onboarding;
- Arabic/English license certificate and sales materials;
- reviewed bilingual agreement templates;
- English master legal agreement with a lawyer-reviewed Arabic translation;
- UAE-specific privacy, electronic signature, commercial licensing and payment workflow review.

## Partner qualification
Verify UAE trade license, legal entity, beneficial ownership, authorized signatory, sector experience, customer references, compliance procedures and ability to report downstream sales. Do not grant exclusive country rights until minimum sales and service obligations are demonstrated.

## Claims and disclaimers
Never claim government approval, guaranteed legal enforceability, authenticity of goods, proof of title or payment finality solely because Cord has recorded an event. Identify each external evidence provider and its trust level.

## Ownership
The commercial licensor name must remain a placeholder until the legal entity and IP assignment chain are confirmed. “Cord by Hantera by KentPH” is project attribution, not sufficient evidence of corporate title.
