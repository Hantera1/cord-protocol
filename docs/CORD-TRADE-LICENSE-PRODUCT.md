# Cord Trade License v1 — Product Definition

**Owner model:** a new, separate legal entity (the “Cord Trade License Company”), to be incorporated/selected after jurisdiction and tax review.
**First market:** United Arab Emirates, with Dubai as the first sales focus.
**Product:** agreement templates + Cord Trade software/workflows.
**Status:** product definition; no company has yet been incorporated and no rights have yet been transferred.

## 1. What is sold

A commercial package combining:
1. A controlled library of trade-agreement templates and schedules.
2. A right to use Cord Trade software features for agreement workflow, evidence packaging, verification and audit exports.
3. Optional onboarding, customisation, training and support.
4. Optional partner/reseller rights under a separate written grant.

The package is not legal advice and the templates are not guaranteed to be enforceable for every transaction or jurisdiction. Customers must obtain local legal advice for their use case.

## 2. Product SKUs

| SKU | Customer | Grant | Default commercial term |
|---|---|---|---|
| CORD-TRADE-STARTER | One legal entity | Internal use, template library and core Trade workflow | Annual |
| CORD-TRADE-BUSINESS | Active trading company | Internal use, larger volume and audit exports | Annual |
| CORD-TRADE-GROUP | Corporate group | Named affiliated entities | Annual |
| CORD-TRADE-RESELLER | Approved channel partner | Market and resell approved subscriptions | 2-year initial term, performance review |
| CORD-TRADE-WHITE-LABEL | Qualified enterprise | Limited rebranding rights | Individually negotiated |

Prices are intentionally not fixed in this technical specification. Before publication, approve price lists in AED and SEK, VAT/tax handling, transaction caps, support tiers, renewal pricing and partner commission.

## 3. Initial vertical agreement packs

Launch with only two reviewed packs:
- **High-value goods sale:** watches, jewellery, art and collectibles; identity/authority, item schedule, serial/reference, condition, certificate references, payment and delivery.
- **Precious metals sale:** metal/lot description, weight/unit, fineness, assay report, origin declarations, custody, payment, delivery and dispute process.

Next packs (not v1): wholesale purchase/supply, consignment/auction, services, digital goods and cross-border distribution.

Templates must be modular and clearly label optional clauses, jurisdiction-specific provisions, commercial variables and counsel review notes. Avoid implying that a template itself verifies item authenticity, title, payment or delivery.

## 4. Licensing grant

Default Business License:
- non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable;
- limited to named licensee legal entity and approved territory;
- internal business use only;
- term-limited and subject to payment;
- no right to copy/resell templates, operate a bureau, provide third-party legal services, distribute software or issue Cord licenses;
- no right to remove copyright, trademark, authorship or attribution notices;
- no reverse engineering except where mandatory law permits;
- no use of Cord marks except approved marketing.

Reseller and white-label rights are separate SKUs and require separate terms. No implied exclusivity.

## 5. Release contents

Each release must include:
- versioned agreement pack and change log;
- English master version and separately reviewed Arabic translation;
- usage guide and jurisdiction warning;
- matching digital workflow version;
- test examples and evidence export;
- license certificate and entitlement list;
- support and renewal terms.

## 6. Customer journey

1. Customer selects product/vertical pack.
2. Sales qualifies legal entity, country, intended use and expected volume.
3. Legal/compliance reviews higher-risk customer/partner and territory.
4. Quote and order are issued with stable IDs.
5. Customer signs master license agreement and applicable order form.
6. Finance confirms payment or approved credit.
7. License service issues signed certificate and activates access.
8. Customer receives onboarding and approved templates.
9. Usage, renewal, support and partner activity are monitored.
10. On expiry/termination, new access stops; customer can export its own signed contracts and evidence, subject to legal retention and privacy obligations.

## 7. UAE launch constraints

- Confirm the exact UAE business activity and whether it is software licensing, document/template sales, consultancy, reseller activity or a combination. Obtain advice from the selected licensing authority before incorporating.
- Do not call Cord a licensed trust service provider or issue qualified e-signatures, seals or timestamps. Integrate a TDRA-licensed provider where the product requires regulated trust services.
- Arabic translation must be reviewed by UAE-qualified counsel; agree language precedence.
- Use UAE counsel for commercial contract, consumer, privacy, cross-border data, IP and tax questions.
- Start with a direct pilot or non-exclusive reseller; no master distribution or exclusive territory at launch.

## 8. Product success metrics

- qualified leads and conversion by vertical;
- signed paid licenses;
- activation time;
- active licensees and renewal rate;
- agreement pack usage and completion;
- verified evidence exports per license;
- partner-sourced revenue and reporting accuracy;
- support cost per license;
- disputes, refunds and compliance exceptions.
