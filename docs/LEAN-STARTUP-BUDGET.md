## Lean operating policy
- No employee payroll at incorporation.
- No UAE subsidiary or office until a paid pilot requires it.
- No custom CRM: start with a controlled low-cost business system and exportable ledger.
- No public registry of customer personal data.
- No paid reseller commissions before sales are invoiced and collected.
- No customer license activation before signed agreement and payment/approved credit.
- Budget separately for accounting, bank, insurance, privacy/security, Arabic translation, UAE legal advice and domain/email.

## Capital maintenance
Share capital may be spent on legitimate business costs after registration, but it is not free cash. Monitor equity monthly. Swedish limited companies have statutory obligations if equity falls below half of registered share capital; obtain accountant advice immediately if this risk arises.
