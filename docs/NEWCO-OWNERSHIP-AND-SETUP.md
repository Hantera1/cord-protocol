# Cord Trade License Company — Ownership and Setup Decision

**Decision recorded:** establish a new separate company to be the commercial licensor.
**Not yet decided:** country of incorporation, legal name, shareholders, director, registered office, capital, business activity or tax residence.
**Not yet completed:** incorporation, IP assignment, trademark filing, bank account or tax registrations.

## 1. Recommended legal structure

Create a dedicated company (working name: **Cord Trade Licensing AB** if incorporated in Sweden; name subject to availability and registration). It should own or hold only the commercial rights actually assigned to it and contract with customers and channel partners.

Do not automatically incorporate in Dubai solely because Dubai is the first market. Compare:
- Swedish AB as initial licensor, selling cross-border into UAE;
- UAE mainland or free-zone entity as local licensor/distributor;
- Swedish parent/IP company plus UAE sales subsidiary later.

Select only after written advice on tax residence, permanent establishment, VAT, UAE corporate tax, withholding, substance, banking, activity permissions, IP ownership and transfer pricing.

## 2. Recommended staged choice

**Stage A — now:** establish a Swedish project company only if Swedish accountant/lawyer confirms it fits the ownership, funding and tax plan. It can hold the commercial templates, software/service rights and brand rights assigned to it, and contract with UAE customers where legally permitted.

**Stage B — after UAE demand:** establish or appoint a properly licensed UAE operating/distribution entity only when there is a real commercial reason (local staff, local contracting, local invoicing, procurement requirements, partner operations or regulatory requirements).

This is a planning recommendation, not a legal or tax opinion. A free-zone company is not automatically tax-free; UAE corporate tax applies to businesses including free-zone businesses, subject to statutory rules and qualifying conditions.

## 3. Before incorporation

- Decide shareholders and beneficial owners.
- Choose jurisdiction and legal form.
- Confirm exact licensed activity with authority.
- Check name availability and trademark conflicts.
- Prepare articles/constitutional documents and shareholder agreement.
- Appoint directors/manager and authorized signatories.
- Establish registered address and capital requirements.
- Open business bank account.
- Register tax accounts and accounting controls.
- Document related-party agreements and IP assignment price/valuation if rights move from a founder or another entity.

## 4. Intellectual property transfer

The new company cannot own rights merely because it is named as licensor in a website or GitHub file. Complete:
1. Inventory of all relevant code, documentation, schemas, templates, translations, designs, logos, domains and data.
2. Contributor/contractor list and pre-existing materials.
3. Review of Apache 2.0 and all other open-source/third-party obligations.
4. Written assignment from each actual rights holder, with specific work schedules, territory, duration and rights granted as permitted by law.
5. Moral rights/consents to the extent legally possible.
6. Consideration/valuation, tax/accounting treatment and board approvals.
7. Signed assignment and evidence archive.
8. Update repository notices, company IP register and trademark ownership only after transfer is effective.

Cord Core remains available under its existing Apache 2.0 license. The company may own assigned copyrights in original contributions only to the extent the chain of title supports it, but it cannot revoke the rights already granted to recipients under Apache 2.0.

## 5. Brand protection

- Conduct clearance searches for CORD, CORD TRADE and logo in Sweden/EU and UAE.
- Identify appropriate Nice classes with a trademark professional, likely including software/SaaS and relevant business/document services; do not guess final classes.
- File in the name of the actual rights-owning company.
- Secure domains and social handles under company-controlled accounts.
- Use trademark notices consistently; do not use ® before registration.

## 6. Launch gate

No invoice, license certificate or reseller grant should identify the new company as owner/licensor until:
- company exists and is in good standing;
- licensed activities cover the offered service;
- bank and tax setup is complete;
- IP assignments are signed and reviewed;
- customer agreement and privacy documents are approved;
- license registry and support operations are ready.
