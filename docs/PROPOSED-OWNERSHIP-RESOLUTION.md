# Proposed Group Ownership Resolution — Cord Trade Licensing AB

**Draft for the board/shareholder and legal/accounting review. Not adopted or signed.**

## Decision proposed

1. The group intends to establish a separate Swedish private limited company under the working name **Cord Trade Licensing AB**.
2. Subject to confirmation that **Means Zero Holding AB** is duly registered and able to act as founder/shareholder, Means Zero Holding AB shall subscribe for 100% of the shares in Cord Trade Licensing AB.
3. Cord Trade Licensing AB shall be the intended commercial contracting entity for Cord Trade agreement packs, paid software/services, implementation, support and authorized distribution.
4. The company shall initially focus on Sweden as its legal base and the United Arab Emirates as its first export market, with Dubai as the initial commercial focus.
5. The company shall not provide legal advice, custody customer funds or crypto-assets, operate an exchange, or represent itself as a regulated trust-service provider unless separately authorized and approved.
6. No transfer of intellectual property is effective by this resolution. Each asset requires a documented chain of title and a separate written assignment or license.
7. Cord Core remains under its existing open-source license; commercial rights are limited to separately owned or authorized materials and services.
8. No exclusive UAE distribution rights shall be granted before a paid pilot, partner due diligence and separate board approval.
9. The board is authorized to obtain name clearance, draft formation documents, request accounting/legal advice and prepare a lean budget. Incorporation and material commitments require the appropriate formal corporate decisions.
