# Swedish NewCo Establishment Plan — Cord Trade License

**Selected direction:** Sweden; lean start; Cord brand; UAE first market.
**Working company name:** Cord Trade Licensing AB (subject to Bolagsverket approval and name/trademark clearance).
**Status:** planning document, not a registration or legal/tax opinion.

## 1. Recommended structure

Preferred ownership:
- Kent's existing/planned parent company **Means Zero Holding AB**: 100% of Cord Trade Licensing AB.
- Cord Trade Licensing AB: commercial licensor and operator for Cord Trade templates, paid software/services, partner contracts and UAE sales.
- Cord protocol/Core remains subject to its existing open-source license; no attempt is made to revoke Apache 2.0 grants.

This preserves the previously discussed group strategy where Means Zero Holding AB is the overarching parent. Do not assume the holding company exists or is registered until its registration certificate and current share register are checked.

**Lean sequencing rule:** If Means Zero Holding AB is already registered and in good standing, have it subscribe for all shares in the new company from day one. If it is not yet registered, pause before signing the new-company formation documents and compare the cost/timing of forming the holding company first versus another structure with an accountant. Avoid creating Cord Trade Licensing AB personally and transferring it later without tax/legal advice.

## 5. Lean incorporation budget

Current published starting figures from official Swedish sources:
- Minimum share capital: SEK 25,000.
- Bolagsverket registration fee via e-service: from SEK 2,400.
- Beneficial-owner registration: SEK 250.
- Swedish trademark application via PRV e-service: SEK 2,700 for one class; SEK 1,000 per additional class.

Indicative cash requirement:
- Company formation and beneficial owner: SEK 27,650 (25,000 share capital + 2,400 registration + 250 beneficial-owner fee), excluding bank fees and professional help.
- If adding one Swedish trademark class immediately: indicative total SEK 30,350, excluding clearance, additional classes and counsel.

Share capital is not a government fee. Once registered, it can be used for legitimate company expenses, but the company must maintain adequate equity. If more than half the registered share capital is consumed, special rules may require a control balance sheet and action.

Lean recommendation: incorporate first, validate product and ownership, and defer trademark filing until clearance and class selection are complete. Do not spend on UAE entity formation, office, employees, custom platform or exclusive distribution before a paid pilot.

## 6. Formation sequence

1. Confirm Means Zero Holding AB registration status, shareholders, board, F-tax/VAT and bank account.
2. Confirm exact new company name with Bolagsverket name guidance and preliminary company-name/trademark searches.
3. Decide registered seat, board member/deputy, financial year, share count and share capital.
4. Draft and approve stiftelseurkund and bolagsordning; holding company signs as founder/subscriber.
5. Pay share capital to bank and obtain bank certificate.
6. Submit registration through verksamt.se and pay Bolagsverket fee.
7. Prepare share register immediately after incorporation.
8. Register beneficial owner and confirm who ultimately controls the company.
9. Apply for F-tax and VAT as relevant; no employer registration until employing staff.
10. Set up accounting, business bank account, invoice series, insurance, domain and company email.
11. Sign IP assignment and intercompany license/transfer documents after rights inventory and tax review.
12. Complete customer agreement, privacy documentation and license registry before first sale.

## 7. Accounting and tax controls

- Separate bank account and bookkeeping from all other group companies.
- Written intercompany agreements for IP, development, support, shared staff and cost allocations.
- Market-based pricing and transfer-pricing documentation where required for cross-border/related-party dealings.
- Confirm VAT treatment for software, digital services and templates sold to UAE business customers; do not assume all supplies have the same place-of-supply treatment.
- Confirm corporate tax and permanent-establishment implications before a UAE partner or local staff can bind the Swedish company.
- Corporate tax for Swedish AB is currently 20.6%; verify current rules with accountant.

## 8. No-go gates

Do not issue a paid license or claim the new company owns Cord until:
- the new company is registered;
- the actual shareholder and beneficial owner are documented;
- rights in each template, code contribution, translation, logo and domain are inventoried;
- all necessary assignments and third-party permissions are signed;
- commercial terms, privacy roles and UAE legal review are complete;
- invoicing, payment confirmation and license activation are controlled.
