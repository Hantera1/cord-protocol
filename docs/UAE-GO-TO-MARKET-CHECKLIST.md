# UAE Go-to-Market Checklist — Cord Trade License

## Target launch
A controlled UAE pilot focused on Dubai. First customer segments:
1. luxury watch/jewellery dealers;
2. precious-metal and bullion traders;
3. auction houses and professional resellers;
4. B2B import/export and wholesale businesses.

## Offer
Lead with two practical packs:
- High-Value Goods Sale Pack (English/Arabic after legal review).
- Precious Metals Sale Pack (English/Arabic after legal review).

Demonstrate an end-to-end transaction: draft terms → counterparties → signatures through an external provider → payment reference → delivery/assay evidence → exportable Cord evidence package.

## Partner selection
Prefer one non-exclusive design/implementation partner with:
- UAE trade license matching actual activity;
- transparent beneficial ownership and authorized signatory;
- experience with the target sector;
- credible business references;
- ability to support Arabic and English;
- willingness to report every lead, quote, sale, renewal and cancellation;
- no demand for blanket exclusivity at pilot stage.

## 90-day sequence

### Days 1–30: legal and product readiness
- choose licensor jurisdiction and incorporate only after advice;
- sign IP assignments and establish ownership register;
- clear brand and start trademark filing;
- select UAE activity and licensing route with local advisor;
- finalize English master terms and lawyer-reviewed Arabic translation;
- create demo packs with synthetic data only.

### Days 31–60: partner and pilot
- shortlist 10–15 potential design partners;
- conduct 5–8 discovery meetings;
- select one pilot partner/customer;
- sign NDA, pilot agreement and data processing terms as appropriate;
- configure a small private license registry;
- test onboarding, license activation, payment reconciliation and revocation.

### Days 61–90: controlled commercial launch
- convert pilot to paid license if acceptance criteria pass;
- issue first signed license certificate;
- publish Arabic/English product pages with accurate claims;
- measure lead conversion, license activation, usage, renewal intent and support burden;
- review partner reporting and commission reconciliation.

Targets are planning goals, not guaranteed outcomes.

## Mandatory UAE legal checks
- company/business activity and local presence;
- UAE Commercial Companies and relevant free-zone/mainland rules;
- Federal Decree-Law No. 46 of 2021 and Executive Regulation No. 28 of 2023 if providing regulated trust services;
- data protection and cross-border data transfers;
- VAT and corporate tax;
- marketing, consumer and e-commerce requirements;
- AML/sanctions obligations if activities or customers trigger them;
- trademark and copyright ownership/registration.

## Sales claims
Allowed: “creates structured evidence records”, “checks document integrity and signatures against configured trust anchors”, “helps manage trade agreement workflows”.
Not allowed without separate proof/authorization: “government approved”, “legally guaranteed”, “certifies genuine watches/metals”, “guarantees ownership”, “guarantees payment”, “qualified UAE digital signature”.
