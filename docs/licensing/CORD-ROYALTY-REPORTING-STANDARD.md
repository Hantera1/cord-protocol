# Cord Royalty Reporting Standard — UAE

**Status:** Proposed operational standard. Applies only after incorporation of the definitive terms into a signed licence.

## 1. Principle
Royalty is calculated on revenue attributable to the licensed Cord Commercial Assets or licensed Cord-branded products/services. It is not calculated automatically on the full value of a merchant's underlying goods, payment principal, marketplace GMV or every transaction where Cord is mentioned.

## 2. Rate and formula
- Territory: United Arab Emirates.
- Licence: non-exclusive.
- Rate: 2%.
- Formula: 2% × Net Cord-attributable Licence Revenue actually received during the reporting period.

## 3. Revenue attribution
The Product Schedule must identify the revenue streams that qualify, for example:
- standalone Cord verification charges;
- subscription fees for separately licensed Cord Commercial modules;
- fees for issuing or managing a licensed Cord evidence record/certificate;
- licence fees charged by Licensee for a product marketed under the licensed Cord mark.

Revenue from unrelated services, underlying merchandise, payment principal and third-party funds merely passing through Licensee is excluded unless expressly and fairly allocated to a Cord-branded/licensed product in the signed Product Schedule.

## 4. Permitted adjustments
The definitive contract must specify treatment of:
- VAT and similar transaction taxes;
- actual refunds and chargebacks;
- discounts and credits;
- bad debt and uncollected invoices;
- payment-provider fees;
- third-party pass-through costs;
- bundles and mixed products;
- affiliates, resellers and sublicensed distributors;
- multi-territory customers and cross-border sales;
- foreign exchange conversion.

No general overhead, salaries, marketing, rent or corporate income tax deduction is permitted unless expressly negotiated.

## 5. Quarterly report fields
Minimum report:
1. License ID and reporting period.
2. Product/module and product version.
3. Revenue stream and customer category.
4. Count of chargeable units/events, where relevant.
5. Gross Cord-attributable receipts.
6. Each permitted deduction by category.
7. Net Cord-attributable Licence Revenue.
8. Royalty rate and amount.
9. Currency, conversion source and conversion date.
10. Refunds/chargebacks from prior periods.
11. Affiliate/reseller amounts and allocation method.
12. Officer certification that the report is complete and accurate to the best of the signer's knowledge.

Reports should use aggregated business data and must not include unnecessary end-customer personal data, payment credentials, card details or identity documents.

## 6. Audit and records
Keep source records sufficient to reproduce the calculation. Access is limited to relevant financial records and an independent auditor under confidentiality. The agreement sets notice, frequency, scope, retention, cost allocation and treatment of discrepancies.

## 7. Payment controls
The agreement specifies invoicing entity, invoice timing, due date, settlement currency, bank details, bank fees, late interest, tax forms, withholding handling and any gross-up. Do not promise a tax outcome. Obtain cross-border tax advice before execution.

## 8. No direct payment role
Royalty settlement is a business-to-business licence payment. It does not authorise Cord to receive customer funds, operate a payment gateway or provide regulated payment services.
