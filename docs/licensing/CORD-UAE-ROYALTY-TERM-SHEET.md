# Cord UAE Commercial, Trademark and Royalty Licence — Term Sheet

**Status:** Non-binding commercial term sheet for negotiation and UAE/SWeden legal review. It is not an offer capable of acceptance, a signed licence, legal advice, or evidence that the proposed licensor currently owns all relevant rights.

## 1. Proposed parties
- **Licensor:** The legal entity that, at signing, can demonstrate ownership of or authority to license each relevant Commercial Asset and the CORD mark in the UAE. Legal name, registration number, registered address and signatory authority: **to be completed**.
- **Licensee:** UAE-incorporated or otherwise UAE-authorised business entity. Legal name, licence number, registered address and signatory authority: **to be completed**.
- KentPH is a proposed attribution/creator designation only; it is not automatically the contracting licensor.

No agreement may be signed or licence issued until the licensor's legal identity, authority and chain of title are verified.

## 2. Territory and exclusivity
- **Territory:** United Arab Emirates (UAE) only.
- **Exclusivity:** Non-exclusive. Licensor may appoint other licensees and serve other customers in the Territory.
- No rights are granted for the wider GCC, MENA or any other country.
- Licensee must not actively market a UAE-territory licence as authorising use outside the Territory. Online accessibility and cross-border use must be addressed in the definitive agreement and applicable law.

## 3. Licensed scope
Subject to a signed Product Schedule, the licence may cover:
1. identified, separately licensed Cord Commercial extensions/modules and versions;
2. identified commercial SDKs, workflows, templates or documentation, only to the extent Licensor owns or is authorised to license them;
3. a limited, revocable, non-exclusive right to use the CORD and CORD BY KENTPH marks solely with approved licensed products/services in the Territory, subject to valid trademark rights and quality-control rules;
4. approved use of the required attribution statement.

**Excluded:** Cord Core and any component distributed under Apache License 2.0 are not converted into paid or restricted software by this agreement. The agreement does not alter, replace or restrict existing Apache 2.0 permissions. Use of Core alone, without a separately licensed Commercial Asset or licensed mark, does not trigger this royalty.

## 4. Royalty
- **Rate:** 2% (two per cent).
- **Royalty base:** Net Cord-attributable Licence Revenue actually received by Licensee or its controlled affiliates from commercial exploitation of the Licensed Products in the Territory.
- The definitive agreement must define revenue attribution for bundles, subscriptions, reseller arrangements, free trials, discounts, refunds, bad debt, related-party sales and multi-territory sales.
- Unless expressly agreed otherwise, exclude VAT/sales taxes, genuine refunds and chargebacks, and separately itemised third-party pass-through amounts not retained by Licensee. Do not deduct ordinary operating costs, salaries, general overhead or income taxes.
- Payment-provider fees should be treated consistently in the Product Schedule; they are not automatically deductible.
- No royalty is due on unrelated product revenue, the underlying value of goods sold, payment principal, merchant settlement amounts or general marketplace gross merchandise value merely because a Cord event is recorded.
- No minimum guarantee or upfront royalty is proposed for the initial pilot. Any minimum, advance or signing fee requires a separately agreed written amendment.

**Calculation:** Royalty = 2% × Net Cord-attributable Licence Revenue for the reporting period.

## 5. Reporting, invoicing and audit
- Quarterly reports within 30 days after each calendar quarter.
- Reports show product, customer category, transaction/event count, gross Cord-attributable receipts, permitted exclusions, net royalty base, calculation, currency, refunds/chargebacks and affiliate/reseller revenue.
- Payment within 30 days after a valid invoice, subject to agreed tax documentation and bank details.
- Records retained for at least seven years or the period required by applicable law, whichever is longer, subject to data-minimisation requirements.
- Licensor may audit relevant records no more than once per year on reasonable notice through an independent auditor bound by confidentiality. If an underpayment exceeding 5% is found for an audited period, Licensee pays the shortfall and reasonable audit costs; otherwise Licensor bears audit costs.
- Currency conversion source and date must be agreed in the definitive agreement. Proposed reporting currency: AED; settlement currency: to be agreed.

## 6. Attribution and brand use
For each product/service that uses a Licensed Commercial Asset or is marketed under the licensed Cord mark, Licensee must display the approved attribution in the locations and formats set out in the Brand Schedule. Proposed baseline:

> CORD BY KENTPH  
> Licensed Cord Technology  
> Commercial License ID: [issued identifier]  
> Developed by KentPH

The final wording must accurately distinguish the legal licensor, the actual author/rights holder and the brand owner. Attribution must not imply that KentPH or Licensor operates, endorses, audits or guarantees Licensee's payment service.

No attribution obligation is imposed on users solely by virtue of using Apache-licensed Core. No right to use a mark is granted until trademark ownership/authority and any UAE registration requirements are verified.

## 7. Licensee operating model and responsibility allocation
Licensee is responsible for its own product, customer contracts, hosting, operations, security, integrations, customer support, payment service providers, regulatory permissions, tax, consumer disclosures and personal-data processing, except to the extent a mandatory law or the actual parties' conduct imposes a non-delegable duty on Licensor.

Licensee must independently select and contract with appropriately authorised payment service providers. Licensor:
- does not receive, hold, control, transfer or settle customer funds;
- does not provide acquiring, payment initiation, remittance, stored value, exchange, custody or wallet services;
- does not receive card data, payment credentials, seed phrases or identity documents;
- does not host or operate Licensee's customer platform under this licence;
- does not promise payment settlement, chargeback outcomes, delivery, identity, legal title, authenticity, condition or truth of submitted evidence.

Cord may provide protocol specifications and software artefacts as expressly listed. Any support, hosting, data processing or managed service requires a separate written agreement and legal review.

## 8. Technical and product limitations
Cord Core verification establishes only the specified integrity/hash/schema result for supplied evidence. It does not establish the truth of claims or the real-world identity, authority, ownership, authenticity, payment finality or delivery of a person, asset or transaction. Licensee must preserve these distinctions in user interfaces, marketing and customer terms.

## 9. Intellectual property
- Each party retains its pre-existing IP.
- No ownership transfer is implied.
- Licensee receives only the limited rights expressly listed in the Product and Brand Schedules.
- Licensee may not remove copyright, licence or attribution notices from Licensed Commercial Assets.
- Licensee may not register confusingly similar marks, claim ownership of Cord IP, or challenge Licensor's valid rights.
- Improvements, feedback, derivative works and employee/contractor contributions require express treatment in the definitive agreement.
- Licensor warrants only that it has authority to grant the specific rights it actually grants, subject to the negotiated warranties and applicable law.

## 10. Term, renewal and termination
- Initial term: 12 months from the effective date.
- Renewal: successive 12-month periods unless either party gives 60 days' written notice.
- Cure period: 30 days for remediable material breach; immediate suspension/termination may be available for unauthorised brand use, IP misuse, sanctions, fraud, serious security risk or unlawful activity, subject to applicable law and proportionality.
- On expiry/termination, Licensee stops using the Commercial Assets and Cord marks, removes attribution from products no longer licensed, and pays accrued royalties.
- Apache 2.0 rights already granted for Core remain governed by Apache 2.0 and are not terminated by this agreement.

## 11. Compliance and privacy
Each party complies with laws applicable to its own activities. The parties must document actual data flows and controller/processor roles. No party is designated a processor merely by contract label. No personal data is to be transferred to Licensor under this licence. If any such processing is later proposed, it requires prior assessment and a separate data-processing agreement where legally required.

Licensee must not claim Cord is a bank, payment institution, regulated payment service, qualified trust service provider, certification body or government-approved system unless independently and lawfully established.

## 12. Liability and insurance
Definitive terms to be drafted by qualified counsel. Liability caps, excluded losses, indemnities and insurance must be proportionate and cannot exclude liability where prohibited by mandatory law, fraud, wilful misconduct or other non-excludable grounds. No clause can guarantee that Licensor has zero legal responsibility.

## 13. Governing law and dispute resolution
**Open for counsel negotiation.** Proposed starting point: Swedish law for the commercial software licence, with mandatory UAE trademark, regulatory, tax and other local laws applying where relevant. Dispute forum, arbitration seat, language, interim relief and enforcement must be selected after Swedish and UAE counsel review. Do not assume a Swedish-law clause displaces mandatory UAE law.

## 14. Conditions precedent
No binding licence, public licence certificate or royalty entitlement arises until:
1. Licensor's legal entity and signing authority are confirmed.
2. IP chain-of-title is documented for every Licensed Commercial Asset.
3. Cord/KentPH trademark clearance, ownership and UAE protection/filing strategy are approved.
4. Definitive agreement, schedules and attribution artwork are signed.
5. UAE legal and tax review is complete.
6. Licensee's UAE legal identity, business activity and authority are verified.
7. Royalty reporting and invoicing controls are operational.

## 15. Status
This term sheet is for discussion only. Except for any separately signed confidentiality or exclusivity agreement, it creates no obligation to grant or accept a licence. All commercial figures are proposed terms, not a market valuation or revenue forecast.
