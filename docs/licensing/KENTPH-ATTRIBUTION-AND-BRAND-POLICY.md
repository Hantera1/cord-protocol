# KentPH Attribution and Cord Brand Policy

**Status:** Proposed policy for commercial licence schedules. Subject to verified ownership, trademark clearance and legal review.

## 1. Purpose
This policy governs attribution and brand use for separately licensed Cord Commercial Assets and products/services authorised to use the Cord marks. It does not amend the Apache License 2.0 or impose new obligations on users solely because they use Cord Core under Apache 2.0.

## 2. Proposed attribution
Baseline presentation, subject to final brand approval:

> CORD BY KENTPH  
> Licensed Cord Technology  
> Commercial License ID: [unique identifier]  
> Developed by KentPH

The definitive agreement must identify the actual legal licensor and rights holder. "Developed by KentPH" may be used only where factually accurate and authorised. It must not misrepresent the licensor, copyright owner, operator, certifier or payment provider.

## 3. Required placement
For a licensed commercial product:
- product/about screen or equivalent persistent product information;
- public product page or commercial documentation;
- relevant downloadable certificate or evidence report, if the Cord-branded product generates one;
- partner marketing material that expressly markets the licensed Cord product.

The exact placements, minimum legibility and approved logo files are specified in the Brand Schedule. Attribution must not imply endorsement of the licensee's business, payment services or individual transactions.

## 4. Prohibited use
Without separate written approval, Licensee must not:
- remove or obscure the required attribution;
- alter the Cord or KentPH marks;
- register Cord/KentPH or confusingly similar marks, domains or social handles;
- suggest that Cord guarantees truth, authenticity, title, payment settlement, delivery or regulatory compliance;
- describe Licensee as an owner or creator of Cord;
- use the marks outside the Territory or for unapproved goods/services;
- sublicense brand rights.

## 5. Quality control
Licensee must comply with approved brand guidelines, provide reasonable samples of branded use on request, correct misleading or non-compliant use within the agreed cure period, and stop use when the licence expires or terminates. Quality-control rights must be exercised consistently with applicable trademark law.

## 6. Apache Core boundary
Apache 2.0 grants rights to use the licensed Work subject to its terms. It does not grant general permission to use the Licensor's trademarks, except the limited customary origin/NOTICE use stated in the licence. This policy does not add a KentPH attribution condition to every Core use, and it does not remove notices required by Apache 2.0.

A party using only Apache-licensed Core, without a separately licensed Commercial Asset or permission to use the Cord/KentPH marks, is not subject to this commercial attribution policy merely by using Core.

## 7. Rights and approval gate
No brand licence may be issued until the legal owner/authorised licensor is verified, relevant marks are cleared and the UAE registration/recordation route is reviewed. The licensee receives no rights beyond those expressly granted in a signed agreement.
